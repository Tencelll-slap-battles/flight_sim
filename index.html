Shared cockpit and radar

One person hosts and gets a four-letter code. Friends open this same website, type the code and take a seat: Captain, First Officer or ATC. Talk with your real voices on push-to-talk: pilots hear ATC when tuned to a Geneva frequency, and the two pilots share an intercom. Empty seats are filled by the computer.
